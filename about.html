About Coinbasket

A small store for people who want to spend their crypto on real, everyday things.

What we do

Coinbasket sells a hand-picked selection of everyday products: clothing, cosmetics, packaged snacks, home and electronics accessories, and wellness basics.

You pay us in stablecoins. We buy the item from the original seller or a print-on-demand supplier, and it ships directly to your address. We don't hold stock, and we never handle your parcel.

Why crypto only

Many people hold crypto but have no easy way to spend it on ordinary purchases. We built Coinbasket so you can buy real products without a bank card or a bank account.

We use stablecoins so the price you see in dollars is the price you pay, with no swings between checkout and payment.

How we work

We are new and we are small. Every order is checked and placed by a person, not an automated system, so we keep our catalog short and our prices fixed.

We reply to messages quickly, send a tracking number as soon as your order ships, and explain our rules in plain language on the policies page.
