How it works

You pay in crypto. We buy the item with a card. The seller ships it straight to you. Here is each step.

1. Pick a product

Browse the categories, choose an item, and select any options such as size or color. Prices are fixed and shown in US dollars, with flat shipping added. Each order is for one item.

2. Pay in stablecoins

The checkout page shows an exact amount in USDC, the wallet address, and the network to use. The last digits of the amount are unique to your order, so we can match your payment to it. Please send exactly that amount.

Use the right network
Send only on the network shown at checkout. Funds sent on any other network can’t be recovered.
3. Tell us where to ship

Fill in the order form with your name, email, delivery address, and the transaction hash from your wallet. You will get a copy of your answers by email.

4. We confirm and place your order

We check your payment on the blockchain, confirm the item is still available, and buy it from the original seller or print-on-demand supplier. You get an email saying your payment was received and the order was placed.

5. It ships to your door

The seller sends the item directly to your address, and we email you the tracking number once it ships. Print-on-demand items such as clothing often take 1 to 3 weeks. Other items usually arrive sooner.

Good to know

We launch in the United States first, and ship to US addresses only.

If we can’t fulfill an order, we refund you in crypto. Underpayments must be topped up, and overpayments are refunded or credited.

Your details are used only to fulfill your order. Read the full policies for shipping, refunds, prohibited items, and privacy.
