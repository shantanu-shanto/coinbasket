Policies

Plain-language rules for shipping, payments, refunds, prohibited items, and privacy. Questions? Use the email address at the bottom of any page.

Shipping · Payments · Refunds · Prohibited items · Privacy

Shipping

We currently ship to addresses in the United States only.

Items are bought from the original seller or a print-on-demand supplier and shipped directly to you. We do not hold stock, and we never receive or reship your parcel.

Shipping is a flat fee per item, shown on each product page and added at checkout.

Print-on-demand items such as clothing are made after you order and often take 1 to 3 weeks to arrive. Other items usually arrive sooner. Delivery times are estimates, not guarantees.

Once your order ships, we email you the tracking number.

Payments

We accept stablecoin payments only, on the coin and network shown at checkout. Prices are in US dollars.

Send the exact amount shown, including the extra digits. Those digits identify your order. After paying, submit the order form with your delivery address and the transaction hash from your wallet. We start your order after we verify the payment on the blockchain.

Wrong network = lost funds
Crypto payments can’t be reversed. Funds sent on a network other than the one shown at checkout may be unrecoverable, and we can’t get them back for you.

Underpayment: if you send less than the amount shown, we will ask you to send the difference before we place your order.

Overpayment: we will refund the extra amount or credit it to a future order, whichever you prefer.

Refunds

If we can’t fulfill your order, for example because the item is out of stock or no longer available, you get a full refund of what you paid.

If your item arrives damaged or defective, or never arrives, contact us with your order details and, where relevant, photos. We review each case individually and will repair it with a replacement or a refund.

Refunds are sent manually in the same stablecoin, to a wallet address you give us, on the network we agree on with you. Please double-check the address and network, because refunds are also irreversible. Network fees may be deducted.

Because print-on-demand items are made specifically for you, we can’t accept returns for a change of mind or a wrong size choice.

Prohibited items

We do not sell, and will not source on request:

  • Medicine, prescription or over-the-counter
  • Alcohol, tobacco, and vape products
  • Weapons and weapon accessories
  • Counterfeit or replica goods
  • Perishable or refrigerated food, fresh produce, meat, and dairy
  • Anything that requires a licence to sell or ship

We only list items from our own catalog. If you ask for something outside it, we will politely decline.

Privacy

We collect your name, email, delivery address, order details, and transaction hash, and a phone number only if the carrier requires one.

We use this information only to verify your payment, place and track your order, and contact you about it. We share your name, address, and any required contact details with the supplier and carrier that deliver your order. We do not sell your data.

Order details are kept in a private record that only we can access, and we keep them as long as we need them for order support and our own records.

To ask about or request deletion of your details, email us.
